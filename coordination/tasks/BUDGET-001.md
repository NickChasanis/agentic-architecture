# BUDGET-001 — Agree troubleshooting and review budgets

Packet revision: 1. Current status and assignment authority: [BOARD.md](../BOARD.md).

## Outcome and context

Translate the accepted preference for worker troubleshooting freedom into measurable operating limits. Read [authority and budgets](../../07-authority-model-policy-and-assumptions.md), [role metrics](../../06-tmux-agent-roles-and-metrics.md), and [communication/freshness](../../08-shared-task-state-and-communication.md).

## Acceptance obligations

- BUD-01: select monetary accounting units and separate elapsed time from active human effort; mark unavailable usage data explicitly.
- BUD-02: set initial task and escalation limits appropriate to the selected setting, with no fixed two-attempt rule.
- BUD-03: specify progress checkpoint cadence and conditions for immediate escalation or revalidation.
- BUD-04: define independent review coverage and which integration checks are mandatory.
- BUD-05: record the owner's approval of consequential cost/latency tradeoffs and who may authorize budget extensions.

Preferences can be discussed alongside SETTING-001; final numeric budgets should reflect its selected scope. No specific price, model, deadline, or spending authorization is assumed. Verification is a decision review against BUD-01–05. The coordinator assigns output ownership and a discussion budget before dispatch; handoff references accepted values and unresolved measurement limits.

## Proposed comparison budget (pending owner approval)

For the next controlled comparison, the coordinator proposes 90 active worker minutes per task with a 15-minute checkpoint, 30 active coordinator/reviewer minutes before escalation, and no paid API usage until the accounting basis and provider limits are explicitly accepted. Workers retain room to troubleshoot while producing new evidence; repeated unchanged attempts, breaking contract changes, missing infrastructure or any budget extension escalate to the human owner. See [controlled comparison](../../15-controlled-comparison.md).
